Why Global Brands Choose SDF
as Their Apparel Manufacturer

From tech pack to FOB delivery — complete clothing manufacturing under one roof in Dhaka.

SDF Clothing manufactures premium apparel for fashion brands, retailers, and startups across the EU, UK, USA, and Australia. Our garments factory in Baridhara DOHS, Dhaka has operated since 1998 with a focus on quality and ethical standards.

As full-package clothing manufacturers, we handle the complete supply chain: fabric sourcing, dyeing, finishing, cut & sew, embroidery, screen printing, AQL inspection, and FOB delivery.

We hold 13 international certifications including GOTS and OEKO-TEX. Our full certification list is available online.

Clothing Manufacturing FAQs

Standard lead time is 30–45 days for bulk orders up to 50,000 pieces. Sample development takes 7–14 days. Use our free Lead Time Calculator for an exact estimate.
